Our Story

Your Neighborhood
Barber Shop

El Barber Shop has been part of Irving's Belt Line community for five years. What started as one chair and a vision has grown into a destination for anyone who values a great haircut and a genuine welcome.

Our barbers bring experience, steady hands and an eye for what works with your face shape, hair type and personal style. We speak English and Spanish, and we treat every client — walk-in or appointment — like a regular.

This is a place where the music is good, the conversation flows and you leave looking and feeling your best.
